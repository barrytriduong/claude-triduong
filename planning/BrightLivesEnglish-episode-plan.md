# Bright Lives English — Episode Plan EP05–EP100

*96 episodes planned on 28 September 2026. Levels alternate: 20 × A1, 28 × A2, 28 × B1, 20 × B2. Every video word, lesson word and target structure is new: none repeats inside this plan or appears in `vocabulary-log.md` (EP01–EP04). The two people who appear twice (Marie Curie, Albert Einstein) have a different story at a different level.*

**How to use:** pick the next episode, then either open its full file in `episodes/` (EP05 and EP06 are written) or ask Claude: *"Write the full episode file for EPNN from the plan"*. Before writing a script, re-check the facts in its fact-check table and confirm the words are still unused in the vocabulary log.

**People:** mostly historical figures. Living people (Temple Grandin, Nick Vujicic, Ruby Bridges, Tu Youyou) are told through positive, well-documented stories only. No politicians, religious leaders, or people at the centre of current controversies.

### EP05 — Temple Grandin (B1)
- **Working title:** Doctors Said She Would Never Talk… Then She Changed How Farms Treat Animals | Temple Grandin | Learn English B1
- **Years:** born 1947 · living: positive story only
- **Story angle:** At two she didn't speak, and doctors suggested sending her away to an institution. Her mother refused and found teachers and speech therapy. Temple thinks in pictures. She became a professor of animal science and designed calmer, kinder systems for cattle.
- **5 video words:** **autism** (noun) · **institution** (noun) · **calm** (verb) · **cattle** (noun) · **design** (verb)
- **Lesson extras (website only):** speech therapy · ranch · professor
- **Target structure:** would + verb for the future in the past (said / thought she would…) — *Doctors said she would never talk.*
- **Topics:** Disability, Science, Women

### EP08 — Fridtjof Nansen (B2)
- **Working title:** He Skied Across Greenland… Then Gave Refugees a Passport | Fridtjof Nansen | Learn English B2
- **Years:** 1861–1930
- **Story angle:** An explorer and scientist, he crossed the Greenland ice cap on skis in 1888 and drifted toward the North Pole in his ship Fram. After the First World War he led efforts to bring prisoners of war home and created the "Nansen passport" for stateless refugees, winning the Nobel Peace Prize in 1922.
- **5 video words:** **refugee** (noun) · **stateless** (adjective) · **drift** (verb) · **expedition** (noun) · **humanitarian** (adjective)
- **Lesson extras (website only):** ice cap · prisoner of war · peace prize
- **Target structure:** inversion: Not only did he …, but he also … — *Not only did he cross Greenland, but he also helped hundreds of thousands of prisoners return home.*
- **Topics:** Exploration, Kindness
