- **Target structure:** *would* + verb for the future in the past (after *said / thought / believed*) — "Doctors said she would never talk."

| # | Word | Part of speech | Meaning (B1) | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **autism** | noun | a difference in how a person's brain works, which can change how they talk, learn and feel sounds and touch | Temple is autistic: she has autism. |
| 2 | **institution** | noun | a large building where people live and are looked after, often far from their families | Doctors wanted to send her to an institution. |
| 3 | **calm** | verb (past: calmed) | to make a person or an animal feel less worried or afraid | Deep pressure calmed her. |
| 4 | **cattle** | noun (plural) | cows and bulls that people keep on farms | She watched the cattle on her aunt's ranch. |
| 5 | **design** | verb (past: designed) | to plan how something will look and work | She designed new systems for farms. |

**Structure (end of scene 7):**
> **would + verb** — the future *seen from the past*
> *They say she **will** never talk.* → *They **said** she **would** never talk.*
> *Doctors said she **would** never talk.*

## Voiceover script (paste into Vids exactly, one block per scene)

**Scene 1 — Hook** (hook visuals only, no title text)
[slowly] When she was two, she didn't speak. Doctors said she would never talk, and that she should live in an institution. Her mother said no. This is the story of Temple Grandin.

**Scene 2 — Welcome** (logo + tagline, as EP03)
[slowly] Hello, and welcome to Bright Lives English. Real stories. Real English. Today's level is B1. In this story, listen for five new words.

**Scene 3 — Word 1: autism** (on screen: *autism · noun · a difference in how a person's brain works*)
[slowly] Autism. Autism is a difference in how a person's brain works. It can change how people talk, learn, and feel sounds and touch. Temple is autistic.

**Scene 4 — Word 2: institution** (on screen: *institution · noun · a large building where people live and are looked after*)
[slowly] Institution. An institution is a large building where people live and are looked after, often far from their families. Doctors wanted to send her to an institution.

**Scene 5 — Word 3: calm** (on screen: *calm · verb · to make someone feel less worried or afraid* · past: calmed)
[slowly] Calm. When you calm someone, you help them feel less worried or afraid. The past is calmed. Deep pressure calmed her.

**Scene 6 — Word 4: cattle** (on screen: *cattle · noun, plural · cows and bulls on a farm*)
[slowly] Cattle. Cattle are cows and bulls that people keep on farms. It's a plural word: we say "the cattle are". She watched the cattle on her aunt's ranch.

**Scene 7 — Word 5: design + structure** (on screen: *design · verb · to plan how something will look and work* · past: designed; then the structure box)
[slowly] Design. When you design something, you plan how it will look and work. She designed new systems for farms. Now, one pattern: would, for the future in the past. Doctors said she would never talk. Ready? Let's begin.

> ⏱ This is the longest voiceover (about 19–20 s). If Vids makes it longer than 20 s, move "Now, one pattern… Let's begin." into its own scene (as in the A1 format) or add a matching still.

**Scene 8 — A different child** (key word: **autism** — disclaimer at the bottom: *Illustrations are artistic interpretations, not real photos of Temple Grandin.*)
[slowly] Temple Grandin was born in Boston, in the United States, in 1947. As a small child, she didn't speak. Loud sounds hurt her ears, and she hated being hugged.

**Scene 9 — "She will never talk"** (key word: **institution**)
[slowly] When she was two, doctors said she had brain damage. They said she would never talk, and that she should go to an institution. But her mother refused, and found her a speech teacher.

**Scene 10 — School** (key word: **autism**)
[slowly] Slowly, Temple learned to talk. At school, other children laughed at her, and life was very hard. But a science teacher, William Carlock, believed in her and showed her how exciting science could be.

**Scene 11 — The ranch** (key word: **cattle**)
[slowly] At fifteen, she spent a summer on her aunt's ranch in Arizona. She watched the cattle go into a squeeze chute, a machine that held them tightly. The pressure calmed the animals.

**Scene 12 — The squeeze machine** (key word: **calmed**)
[slowly] Temple had an idea. At eighteen, with help from her teacher, she built her own squeeze machine. When she felt anxious, the deep pressure calmed her too.

**Scene 13 — Thinking in pictures** (key word: **cattle**)
[slowly] Temple thinks in pictures. She can walk through a farm in her mind, like watching a film. She understood that cattle, like her, were afraid of small things: shadows, sudden noises and quick movements.

**Scene 14 — A world of men** (key word: **designed**)
[slowly] In the 1970s, the cattle business was a world of men. Few people believed she would succeed. But she designed calmer, curved paths for cattle. Today, almost half of the cattle in North America are handled in a system she designed.

> ⏱ About 20 s. If it runs long, split after "…she would succeed." (scene 14a/14b) or add a still.

**Scene 15 — Professor Grandin** (key word: **would**)
[slowly] Temple became a professor at Colorado State University. The girl who doctors said would never talk now speaks to audiences all over the world, about animals and about autism.

**Scene 16 — Review**
[slowly] Let's review. Temple is autistic. Doctors wanted to send her to an institution. Deep pressure calmed her. She watched the cattle. And she designed new systems for farms. And remember: doctors said she would never talk.

**Scene 17 — Message + call to action**
[slowly] Temple's story teaches us that a different mind can see what others miss. Want more practice? Go to brightlivesenglish.com for flashcards, a worksheet and a quiz. Please subscribe. See you in the next true story!

**Scene 18 — End card**
Logo on cream (no voiceover), music only, 4–5 seconds. Keep from EP03.
